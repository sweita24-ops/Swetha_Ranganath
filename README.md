## 🏆 Accomplishments & Career Highlights

### Advanced Reasoning in Copilot Chat

- Led the 0→1 build of Advanced Reasoning in Copilot Chat, driving execution across Design, Engineering, and Science to launch to Frontier and worldwide (WW) rings.
- Established early customer signal showing measurable improvement in task completion compared with the baseline.
- Launched new capabilities, including skills and actions; Teams, Calendar, and email actions; integrations with other MCPs and data sources.
- Defined a new user elicitation model to help people express what they want to accomplish and get things done directly in chat.
- Public reference: [Use Copilot Chat for long-running, multi-step work](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/use-copilot-chat-for-long-running-multi-step-work)

### Scheduled Tasks in Copilot

- Currently working on scheduled tasks in Copilot.
- Defined hero scenarios and the user experience for Copilot to act at the right time and help users accomplish what matters most to them.
- Released to internal company rings, with plans to scale to Frontier customers shortly.

### Microsoft 365 Copilot Tuning

- Founded M365 Copilot Tuning, Microsoft's no-code fine-tuning platform that enables enterprises to customize foundation models without requiring machine-learning expertise.
- Featured in the CEO's Build keynote.
- Public reference: [Microsoft 365 Copilot Tuning overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/developer/copilot-tuning-overview)

### Product & Platform Leadership

- Shaped AI experiences for Microsoft 365 Copilot, helping define how enterprise users interact with AI in real work contexts.
- Built and scaled agentic AI capabilities that support multi-step reasoning, orchestration, and enterprise workflow execution.
- Led product thinking around Copilot platform extensibility, making it easier to integrate AI into business processes and internal tooling.
- Worked across research, product, design, and engineering to connect foundational AI capabilities with practical enterprise needs.

### AI Systems & Model Innovation

- Contributed to enterprise AI tuning and adaptation frameworks that help models learn domain-specific operations and user workflows.
- Designed governance and access models to support responsible enterprise adoption of AI systems.
- Advanced AI evaluation practices with grounded assessments, rubric-driven reviews, and benchmark-driven validation.
- Helped bring together human-in-the-loop design with scalable AI system architecture.

### Research, Datasets & Open-Source Impact

- Helped drive the [MASSIVE dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AmazonScience/massive), a multilingual spoken-language understanding benchmark spanning 1M utterances across 51 languages.
- Paper: [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.681/) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08582)

### Ecosystem & Strategy
